ℹ️ How scoring works
  • Speed: full marks at 120–150 wpm, the range most presentation guides and studies recommend; below about 110 starts to drag.
  • Filler words: about 1 per minute is ideal; the average speaker uses about 5. The speech service often hides "um/uh", so your real count may be higher — use Listen back.
  • Pauses: ordinary speech has 4–5 pauses a minute; TED speakers about 5. Planned 2–3 s pauses are fine; only silences of 3 s or more count as hesitation.
  • Structure & Clarity: from the checklist and the restarts/repeats in Coach's feedback.
  • Overall: a weighted average — Speed, Fillers and Structure count most. 85+ = presentation-ready, 70–84 = good, 55–69 = developing.
