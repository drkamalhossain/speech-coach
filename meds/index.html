☁️ Sync with Google Sheet (off)

Do this once, on your laptop. Your records will go to a private Google Sheet that only you can open, and your phone will show the same data.

  1. Open sheets.new (signed in to your Google account). Name the sheet Medicine Log.
  2. In that sheet click Extensions → Apps Script. Delete everything in the code box.
  3. Click and paste it into the code box. Click 💾 Save.
  4. Click Deploy → New deployment. Click the ⚙️ next to “Select type” → Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy.
  5. Click Authorize access → choose your account → Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → Allow. (This warning appears because the script is your own, not a published app.)
  6. Copy the Web app URL and paste it here:
Set up your phone

Click below, send the copied link to yourself (WhatsApp/email), and open it on your phone in Chrome. It connects the phone to the same sheet automatically. Keep this link private.

Records stay on your devices and in your own Google Sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
